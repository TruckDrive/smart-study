Smart Study turns Anki into the one place where you prepare for an exam. It plans your days up to the exam, shows you what is due today, and keeps the tools you reach for while studying right next to your cards: notes, PDFs, mind maps, a browser, an AI assistant, a focus timer and music. Anki itself stays the way you know it. Free and open source. A study plan that reaches your exam
  • Tell it what you have to learn: book pages, lecture videos, task lists and Anki decks, plus your exam date and how long you can study each day.
  • It spreads the work over your days, topic by topic: read, watch, then do the cards. Milestones, lab sessions, days off and Anki's own review forecast are taken into account.
  • It keeps itself up to date: what you do in Anki is ticked off for you. Fell behind? One click replans the rest.
  • For the last stretch: a weak-spot round, a mock exam and a retrospective that shows your real pace.
  • Share a plan with friends as a short code that fits into a chat message. Your progress stays private.
Today: one glance, then start
  • The countdown to your next exam and today's items from your plan, each with a Study button.
  • Three small charts for what is due, what you reviewed and how much you remember, each with its own period.
  • A daily challenge, levels, ranks and a streak, for the days when motivation needs a push.
  • It sits next to your deck list and steps aside while you review.
A workspace next to your cards
  • Notebook and PDFs: nested pages, task lists, a built-in PDF viewer and a card creator for the PDF you are reading.
  • Mind maps with links to your cards.
  • Browser: tabs and tab groups, an ad blocker, PDFs right in the tab, study-plan videos that resume and tick themselves off, picture in picture while you review, and cards from text, images and videos on a page.
  • AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or NotebookLM with your own account, one at a time.
  • Focus timer and music: Pomodoro and session timers with study-time analytics; local tracks, SoundCloud and YouTube Music.
  • Every tool opens from a slim rail on the left and closes again with one click. Switch off the ones you do not need.
Calm by default, yours by choice
  • One quiet look in dark and light, with eight accent colours or a colour of your own, and your own background image if you like.
  • Focus mode: while you review, only the card is shown. Shortcuts and tools keep working.
  • Eleven interface languages, including Persian from right to left.
  • Controller support: study with a gamepad such as the 8BitDo Zero 2.
  • Copy with pictures: select part of a card and it is copied as one image, ready to paste into a chat.
  • Your devices stay in step: plans and settings travel with your normal AnkiWeb sync.
Getting started
  • Install the add-on with the code at the bottom of this page and restart Anki.
  • A short setup asks what you want to use. Only that is switched on; the rest waits in the settings.
  • Open Study Plan in Anki's top bar and create your first plan. Everything else is under Tools → Smart Study.
Suggested add-ons
  • Smart Study ships no other add-ons except the controller support. Under Settings → Add-ons it suggests two that fit well, and one click installs the original from AnkiWeb: Review Heatmap by Glutanimate and Life Drain by Yutsuten.
More from me: free AI study skills Skills for terminal AI agents that work from your own lecture material:
  • ai-study-plan: turns your notes, Moodle videos and Anki decks into a day-by-day study plan up to your exam, written straight into Smart Study.
  • ai-study-notes: turns lecture PDFs into study notes that explain the why, fact-checked against your sources and exported as a clean PDF.
  • ai-study-anki: turns lecture notes into Anki packages with basic, cloze and image occlusion cards, fact-checked against your sources.
  • ai-study-tutor: teaches you your lecture notes step by step like a private tutor, with small chunks and real understanding checks.
  • ai-study-quiz: quizzes you like an oral examiner, one question at a time, with follow-ups and honest grading against your sources.
Good to know
  • Google does not allow "Sign in with Google" inside apps such as Anki. A click on Sign in therefore opens Google's sign-in in a small separate window; Gemini, NotebookLM and YouTube Music then use that sign-in. ChatGPT and Claude: sign in with your e-mail address.
  • Spotify's web player needs a copy protection (Widevine) that Anki's browser does not have, so Spotify plays in its own app or your normal browser.
  • Requires Anki 25.09.4 or newer. Tested on Anki 26.09.
Privacy: no account, no analytics, no update pings. Online connections only happen when you use an online feature yourself (AI, websites, music).
