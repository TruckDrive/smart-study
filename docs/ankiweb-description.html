One Anki add-on for your whole study workflow: a study plan up to your exam, plus a workspace with notebook & PDF viewer, mind maps, focus timer, music, AI assistant, motivation – and the add-ons students install anyway, built in. New in 2.2
  • Settings sync between your devices: your Smart Study settings and the switches for the built-in add-ons travel with your normal AnkiWeb sync, just like your study plans.
  • Add-ons page in the settings: switch each built-in add-on on or off at any time under Settings → Add-ons.
  • Progress bar removed: the built-in progress bar is gone. Want it back? Install Progress bar separately.
New in 2.1
  • Built-in add-ons – no separate installs: copy selections with pictures as an image, review heatmap, controller support and Life Drain. Switch each one on or off under Settings → Add-ons or Tools → Smart Study → Built-in add-ons…
  • Already have one of them installed? Smart Study asks once whether to keep yours or use the built-in one, and takes over your settings.
  • AI assistant with your own account: ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini as tabs – no API key needed.
  • Streaming: YouTube Music in a sidebar; Spotify opens in its app.
  • "What's new" after every update, with a switch next to everything new.
  • Fix: searching in the website sidebar no longer crashes Anki.
Built-in add-ons
  • Copy selections with pictures as an image (on): select part of a card that contains pictures – with the mouse, Ctrl+A or Shift+arrows – and it is copied as one image. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or a chat and the pictures come along; normal pasting only keeps the text. Text-only selections are not touched.
  • Review heatmap (on): a calendar of your reviews and your streak on the deck list, deck overview and statistics.
  • Controller support (on): study with a gamepad such as the 8BitDo Zero 2. Controllers connected before Anki starts are found, and if one cannot be recognised reliably (for example any X-Input pad on Windows) Smart Study asks once which controller it is instead of guessing.
  • Life Drain (off): a life bar that drains while you think and refills when you answer.
Study plan
  • Add what you have to learn – book pages, lecture videos, task lists, Anki decks – plus your exam date and daily study time.
  • Smart Study spreads it over the days until the exam, topic by topic: read, watch, then do the cards. Milestones, lab sessions, days off and Anki's real review forecast are taken into account.
  • Anki items tick themselves off while you study. Fell behind? One click replans the rest.
  • Weak-spot round, mock exam, retrospective with your real pace, maintenance mode. Plans and settings sync between devices through your normal AnkiWeb sync.
Workspace
  • Notebook with a built-in PDF viewer and a PDF card creator
  • Mind maps with links to your cards
  • Pomodoro and session timers with study-time analytics
  • Music: local tracks, SoundCloud, YouTube Music
  • AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with your account, or an API key (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, OpenRouter, DeepSeek) or a local model (Ollama, llama.cpp)
  • Website sidebar, XP, ranks, streaks, daily challenges, deadlines, color themes, ten interface languages
Good to know
  • Google does not allow signing in inside apps such as Anki. Sign in to ChatGPT and Claude with your e-mail address; Gemini and YouTube Music also work without signing in.
  • Spotify's web player needs a copy protection (Widevine) that Anki's browser does not have, so Spotify plays in its own app or your normal browser.
  • Requires Anki 25.09.4 or newer. Tested on Anki 26.09.
Privacy: no account, no analytics, no update pings. Online connections only happen when you use an online feature yourself (AI, websites, music).
