One Anki add-on for your whole study workflow: a study plan up to your exam, plus a workspace with notebook & PDF viewer, mind maps, focus timer, music, AI assistant, more. Free and open source. ✨ New in 2.4: Persian
  • All of Smart Study in Persian, right to left: settings, dashboard, study plan, notebook, mind maps, AI assistant and the built-in add-ons.
  • Anki itself in Persian: a few broken lines in Anki's Persian file make it fall back to English. Smart Study repairs that. Text from inside Anki's backend stays English.
  • The AI assistant can answer in Persian.
📅 Study plan
  • Add what you have to learn – book pages, lecture videos, task lists, Anki decks – plus your exam date and daily study time.
  • Smart Study spreads it over the days until the exam, topic by topic: read, watch, then do the cards. Milestones, lab sessions, days off and Anki's real review forecast are taken into account.
  • Anki items tick themselves off while you study. Fell behind? One click replans the rest.
  • Weak-spot round, mock exam, retrospective with your real pace, maintenance mode. Plans and settings sync between devices through your normal AnkiWeb sync.
🧰 Workspace
  • Notebook with a built-in PDF viewer and a PDF card creator
  • Mind maps with links to your cards
  • Pomodoro and session timers with study-time analytics
  • Music: local tracks, SoundCloud, YouTube Music
  • AI assistant: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with your account, or an API key (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, OpenRouter, DeepSeek) or a local model (Ollama, llama.cpp)
  • Website sidebar, XP, ranks, streaks, daily challenges, deadlines, color themes, eleven interface languages
🧩 Built-in add-ons
  • Copy selections with pictures as an image (on): select part of a card with pictures and it is copied as one image.
  • Review heatmap (on): a calendar of your reviews and your streak on the deck list, deck overview and statistics.
  • Controller support (on): study with a gamepad such as the 8BitDo Zero 2.
  • Life Drain (off): a life bar that drains while you think and refills when you answer.
  • Already have one installed? Smart Study asks once whether to keep yours or use the built-in one. Switch each on or off under Settings → Add-ons.
🤖 More from me: free AI study skills Skills for terminal AI agents that work from your own lecture material:
  • ai-study-plan: turns your notes, Moodle videos and Anki decks into a day-by-day study plan up to your exam, written straight into Smart Study.
  • ai-study-notes: turns lecture PDFs into study notes that explain the why, fact-checked against your sources and exported as a clean PDF.
  • ai-study-anki: turns lecture notes into Anki packages with basic, cloze and image occlusion cards, fact-checked against your sources.
  • ai-study-tutor: teaches you your lecture notes step by step like a private tutor, with small chunks and real understanding checks.
  • ai-study-quiz: quizzes you like an oral examiner, one question at a time, with follow-ups and honest grading against your sources.

💡 Good to know
  • Google does not allow signing in inside apps such as Anki. Sign in to ChatGPT and Claude with your e-mail address; Gemini and YouTube Music also work without signing in.
  • Spotify's web player needs a copy protection (Widevine) that Anki's browser does not have, so Spotify plays in its own app or your normal browser.
  • Requires Anki 25.09.4 or newer. Tested on Anki 26.09.
Privacy: no account, no analytics, no update pings. Online connections only happen when you use an online feature yourself (AI, websites, music).
